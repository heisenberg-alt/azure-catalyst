## The one-paragraph pitch

Azure Catalyst is Microsoft's free, unified service to decide on, plan, and execute migration and modernization to Azure. It discovers your estate (appliance, collector, or file import), builds a CFO-ready business case, assesses readiness and right-sizing, and executes migrations in coordinated waves — with a conversational **Catalyst agent** in Azure Copilot that analyzes inventory, compares strategies, proposes wave plans, and generates deployable landing zone templates.

!!! note "Naming"
    Azure Migrate became Azure Catalyst in November 2026. Existing projects, appliances, and workflows are unaffected, and you may still see both names in the portal during the transition.
